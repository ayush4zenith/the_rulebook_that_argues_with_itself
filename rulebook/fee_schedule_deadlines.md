### Line 14 — Branch Change / Institute Transfer Fee Policy

**Line 14**: Tuition fees paid for institute transfer or internal branch sliding after Round 2 are **strictly non-refundable and non-adjustable against subsequent semesters**. Students are advised to finalize their institution and branch preference before Round 2 closes, as no financial relief will be provided by the Finance Office or the Dean Academics for fees paid in respect of a branch or institute subsequently vacated through a later counselling round or internal transfer mechanism.
