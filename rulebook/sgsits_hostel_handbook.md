# SGSITS Indore — Hostel Handbook
## Academic Year 2025–2026
### Issued by the Chief Warden's Office, SGSITS Indore

---

> **NOTICE**: All hostel residents are bound by the rules contained in this Handbook from the date of room allotment. Ignorance of rules is not an acceptable defence. This Handbook supersedes all previous circulars and notices related to hostel administration. Residents are encouraged to read this document in full and raise queries with the Chief Warden's Office during the induction session held in the first week of each semester.

## Section 1 — Admission to Hostel

### 1.1 Eligibility

1.1.1 Hostel accommodation is available for students enrolled in any year of B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, or MCA programmes at SGSITS Indore, subject to availability of rooms.

1.1.2 Priority for room allotment shall be given in the following order:
(a) First-year B.Tech students (outstation, i.e., from districts other than Indore);
(b) Second-year B.Tech onwards, by merit of CGPA and outstation status;
(c) Postgraduate students.

1.1.3 Day-scholar students (students residing within Indore city limits) are not eligible for hostel accommodation unless the Chief Warden determines that special circumstances warrant an exception.

### 1.2 Room Allotment

1.2.1 Room allotment is carried out by the Chief Warden in consultation with the respective Hostel Wardens. Rooms are allotted for one academic year and must be vacated at the end of the year for re-allotment (unless the student is continuing in the next year and re-applies within the prescribed deadline).

1.2.2 Room sharing arrangements must be accepted as assigned. Students do not have the right to demand a specific room or roommate.

1.2.3 Rooms are provided furnished with a cot, mattress, study table, and chair. Students are responsible for maintaining the condition of the room and its furniture. Any damage to the room or its contents shall be charged to the resident(s) at the time of vacation.

### 1.3 Hostel Caution Deposit

1.3.1 Every hostel resident must deposit a refundable caution deposit of **₹5,000** at the time of admission. This deposit is held against potential damage to hostel property, outstanding mess dues, or any other hostel-related dues.

1.3.2 The caution deposit shall be refunded upon vacating the hostel room, after deducting any outstanding dues. Refund shall be processed within **45 days** of submission of the room vacation clearance form.

## Section 2 — Mess and Dining

### 2.1 Mess Subscription

2.1.1 All hostel residents are required to subscribe to the hostel mess for at least one meal plan per day. Completely opting out of the mess is not permitted for first-year students.

2.1.2 Mess fees are charged on a monthly basis and must be paid by the **5th of each month**. A late fee of **₹50 per day** shall be charged for payments made after the 5th.

2.1.3 The mess menu is published weekly by the Mess Committee, which includes student representatives. Complaints about food quality must be routed through the Mess Committee to the Chief Warden.

### 2.2 Mess Rebate Policy

2.2.1 Students who are away from the hostel for an **officially authorised continuous period of 10 days or more** are eligible for a mess fee rebate for the absent period. The rebate shall be calculated on a per-day basis.

2.2.2 To claim a mess rebate:
(a) The student must submit a written application to the Hostel Warden before leaving, or within 2 days of departure in case of an emergency;
(b) The application must be supported by documentary evidence of the reason for absence (e.g., official letter, medical certificate, travel document);
(c) The application must be approved by the Hostel Warden.

2.2.3 Mess rebate will **not** be granted for periods of less than 10 consecutive days, regardless of the reason. Casual or weekend absences do not qualify.

## Section 3 — Curfew and Entry/Exit Rules

### 3.1 Curfew Timings

3.1.1 All hostel residents must be inside the hostel premises by the following curfew times:

| Hostel | Curfew Time |
|---|---|
| Boys' Hostel (All Years) | 10:00 PM |
| Girls' Hostel (All Years) | 8:00 PM |
| PG / Research Hostel | 10:30 PM |

3.1.2 Curfew timings are enforced through biometric attendance at the hostel gate. The biometric system records entry and exit times. Students who are outside the hostel after the curfew hour will be recorded as "Out After Curfew."

3.1.3 The Girls' Hostel has an additional biometric check at **8:30 PM**, which is the internal curfew for movement within the hostel premises. After 8:30 PM, residents of the Girls' Hostel are expected to be within their room blocks.

### 3.2 Late Entry Permission

3.2.1 Students requiring late entry (i.e., entry after curfew time) on account of institute-sanctioned activities (e.g., technical festivals, cultural events, committee meetings) may apply for a Late Entry Pass from the Dean (Student Welfare) at least **24 hours in advance**.

3.2.2 Late Entry Passes are valid for the specific event and date only and do not constitute a general relaxation of curfew rules.

## Section 4 — Leave Rules for Hostel Residents

### 4.1 Day Leave (Within City)

4.1.1 Hostel residents may take day leave (returning before curfew) without formal permission, but must inform their floor warden verbally before leaving.

### 4.2 Overnight Leave (Local Guardian's Residence)

4.2.1 First-year hostel residents (i.e., students in their first year at SGSITS, studying Semester I or Semester II) shall be treated differently from senior residents for overnight leave purposes.

### Rule 7.2 (Overnight Leave — First-Year Residents)

**Rule 7.2**: First-year hostel residents seeking **overnight leave** must submit a written application **signed by their local guardian** at least **48 hours in advance** to the Chief Warden. The application form must include the guardian's contact number and the address where the student will be staying. Unapproved overnight absence by a first-year resident incurs a **fine of ₹500** per night of unapproved absence, payable to the Hostel Mess Fund.

4.2.2 "Local guardian" for the purposes of Rule 7.2 is defined as any adult (aged 25 or above) residing in Indore city, whose details have been registered with the Institute at the time of admission.

4.2.3 Senior hostel residents (second year and above) may take overnight leave by informing the Hostel Warden on duty and entering their departure and expected return time in the Leave Register maintained at the hostel gate. Senior residents do not require 48-hour advance notice.

### 4.3 Home Leave (Outstation)

4.3.1 Students travelling home (outstation) must submit a Leave Application via the online portal, specifying the dates of departure and expected return. The application must be approved by the HOD or Faculty Advisor.

4.3.2 Home leave during the teaching semester requires that the student's attendance is not thereby brought below the minimum threshold (Chapter 5 of the Academic Ordinance).

## Section 5 — Prohibited Items and Conduct

### 5.1 Prohibited Items in Hostel Rooms

5.1.1 The following items are **strictly prohibited** in hostel rooms:

(a) Electric irons, induction cooktops, electric heaters, and other high-wattage appliances;
(b) LPG cylinders and portable gas stoves;
(c) Motorcycles, scooters, or any motorized vehicles inside the hostel building or courtyard;
(d) Weapons of any kind;
(e) Alcohol, tobacco, or any narcotic substance;
(f) Animals or pets of any kind.

5.1.2 The Hostel Warden may conduct room inspections at any time with reasonable notice. Items found in violation of 5.1.1 shall be confiscated and the resident shall face disciplinary action as per Section 7.

## Section 6 — Internet and Utilities

6.1.1 Internet access is provided through the Institute's Wi-Fi network. The network is filtered and monitored. Attempts to bypass network filters constitute a disciplinary violation.

6.1.2 Electricity is available 24 hours. However, load-shedding schedules apply as per the state electricity board's schedule, and the hostel generator covers essential circuits only (corridors, common areas).

## Section 7 — Disciplinary Rules

### 7.1 Minor Violations

Minor violations include: failing to maintain cleanliness in rooms/common areas, causing noise disturbance after 11:00 PM, and leaving hostel without entry in the leave register.

**Penalty**: Written warning. On the third minor violation, the violation is escalated to a Major violation.

### 7.2 Major Violations

Major violations include: possession of prohibited items, unapproved overnight absence (as per Rule 7.2), physical altercation with fellow residents or staff, and ragging of any kind.

**Penalty**: Financial fine and/or suspension from hostel. The fine amount shall be as prescribed for each specific violation (e.g., ₹500 for unapproved overnight leave per Rule 7.2).

## Section 8 — Curfew Violation Penalties

### Rule 11.4 (Biometric Curfew Violation — Girls' Hostel)

**Rule 11.4**: Any unnotified night absence past the **8:30 PM biometric curfew** in the Girls' Hostel results in **immediate temporary suspension from the hostel for two weeks**, without the provision of any monetary penalty. The suspended student must vacate the hostel room within 24 hours of the suspension order and may re-apply for re-admission to the hostel after the two-week period, subject to availability.

> **[CONTRADICTION POINT C — see contradictions.md]**: Rule 7.2 (Section 4.2 above) specifies a **₹500 financial penalty** for unapproved overnight absence. Rule 11.4 specifies **two-week hostel suspension without any monetary penalty** for absence past the 8:30 PM biometric curfew. Since an unapproved overnight absence necessarily involves being absent past the 8:30 PM curfew, both rules apply to the same factual situation. They prescribe incompatible penalties: one allows monetary fines as the sanction, the other explicitly forbids monetary fines and mandates suspension.

8.1.2 Boys' Hostel curfew violations (i.e., returning after 10:00 PM) are treated as minor violations on the first two occurrences and escalate to major violations (with suspension risk) from the third occurrence.

## Section 9 — Student Insurance

9.1.1 All hostel residents are covered under the Institute's Group Student Accident Insurance Scheme. The scheme covers accidental death and permanent disability arising from accidents that occur within the Institute premises.

9.1.2 The insurance coverage amount is ₹2,00,000 for accidental death and ₹1,00,000 for permanent disability, subject to the terms and conditions of the policy issued by the empanelled insurer.

9.1.3 For claims, students or their parents/guardians must inform the Student Welfare Office within **48 hours** of the accident. Delayed claims may be rejected by the insurer.

---

## Section 10 — Vacating the Hostel

10.1.1 Students must vacate their hostel room within **2 days** of the last End-Semester Examination of the academic year, unless they have received a formal extension from the Chief Warden.

10.1.2 Students who are appearing in supplementary/backlog examinations may apply for a room extension until the last day of the supplementary examination period. Extensions are subject to availability.

10.1.3 Students who withdraw admission from the Institute must vacate the hostel room within **3 working days** of submitting the withdrawal application to the Academic Section.

10.1.4 The caution deposit refund process under Section 1.3.2 is initiated only after the Room Vacation Clearance Form is submitted. No refund shall be processed for rooms not formally vacated.
